Pasado y participio de “withdraw”: withdrew, withdrawn

El verbo “withdraw” (retirar) es irregular: su pasado simple es “withdrew” y su participio es “withdrawn”.

Infinitivo (V1)
withdraw
/wɪˈðdrɔː/
Pasado simple (V2)
withdrew
/wɪˈðdruː/
Participio (V3)
withdrawn
/wɪˈðdrɔːn/

Significado

retirar

to take money out of the bank, or to move back

Ejemplo

She withdrew some money from the bank.

Patrón

ABC — las tres formas son distintas
