Pasado y participio de “become”: became, become

El verbo “become” (volverse) es irregular: su pasado simple es “became” y su participio es “become”.

Infinitivo (V1)
become
/bɪˈkʌm/
Pasado simple (V2)
became
/bɪˈkeɪm/
Participio (V3)
become
/bɪˈkʌm/

Significado

volverse

to start to be something new

Ejemplo

She became a doctor last year.

Patrón

ABA — el infinitivo y el participio son iguales
