Past tense of “foretell”: foretold, foretold

The verb “foretell” is irregular: its past simple is “foretold” and its past participle is “foretold”.

Base form (V1)
foretell
/fɔːˈtɛl/
Past simple (V2)
foretold
/fɔːˈtəʊld/
Past participle (V3)
foretold
/fɔːˈtəʊld/

Meaning

to say what will happen before it happens

Example

The old man foretold the disaster.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
