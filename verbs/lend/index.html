Past tense of “lend”: lent, lent

The verb “lend” is irregular: its past simple is “lent” and its past participle is “lent”.

Base form (V1)
lend
/lɛnd/
Past simple (V2)
lent
/lɛnt/
Past participle (V3)
lent
/lɛnt/

Meaning

to give something to someone for a short time

Example

He lent me his umbrella.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
