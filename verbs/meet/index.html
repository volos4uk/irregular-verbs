Past tense of “meet”: met, met

The verb “meet” is irregular: its past simple is “met” and its past participle is “met”.

Base form (V1)
meet
/miːt/
Past simple (V2)
met
/mɛt/
Past participle (V3)
met
/mɛt/

Meaning

to see and talk to someone

Example

We met at a coffee shop.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
