Past tense of “weave”: wove, woven

The verb “weave” is irregular: its past simple is “wove” and its past participle is “woven”.

Base form (V1)
weave
/wiːv/
Past simple (V2)
wove
/wəʊv/
Past participle (V3)
woven
/ˈwəʊvən/

Meaning

to make cloth by crossing threads

Example

The artist wove a colorful rug.
