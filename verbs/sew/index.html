Past tense of “sew”: sewed, sewn

The verb “sew” is irregular: its past simple is “sewed” and its past participle is “sewn”.

Base form (V1)
sew
/səʊ/
Past simple (V2)
sewed
/səʊd/
Past participle (V3)
sewn
/səʊn/

Meaning

to fix cloth with a needle and thread (sounds just like "sow" — planting seeds — but means something different)

Example

She sewed a button on my coat.

Pattern

ABC — all three forms are different
