Past tense of “think”: thought, thought

The verb “think” is irregular: its past simple is “thought” and its past participle is “thought”.

Base form (V1)
think
/θɪŋk/
Past simple (V2)
thought
/θɔːt/
Past participle (V3)
thought
/θɔːt/

Meaning

to use your mind

Example

I thought about you all day.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
