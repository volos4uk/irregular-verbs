Past tense of “sink”: sank, sunk

The verb “sink” is irregular: its past simple is “sank” and its past participle is “sunk”.

Base form (V1)
sink
/sɪŋk/
Past simple (V2)
sank
/sæŋk/
Past participle (V3)
sunk
/sʌŋk/

Meaning

to go down under the water

Example

The old ship sank quickly.

Pattern

ABC — all three forms are different
