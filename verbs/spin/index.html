Past tense of “spin”: spun, spun

The verb “spin” is irregular: its past simple is “spun” and its past participle is “spun”.

Base form (V1)
spin
/spɪn/
Past simple (V2)
spun
/spʌn/
Past participle (V3)
spun
/spʌn/

Meaning

to turn around and around

Example

The dancer spun around gracefully.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
