Past tense of “light”: lit, lit

The verb “light” is irregular: its past simple is “lit” and its past participle is “lit”.

Base form (V1)
light
/laɪt/
Past simple (V2)
lit
/lɪt/
Past participle (V3)
lit
/lɪt/

Meaning

to make something start to burn or shine

Example

She lit the candles for dinner.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
