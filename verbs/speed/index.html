Past tense of “speed”: sped, sped

The verb “speed” is irregular: its past simple is “sped” and its past participle is “sped”.

Base form (V1)
speed
/spiːd/
Past simple (V2)
sped
/spɛd/
Past participle (V3)
sped
/spɛd/

Meaning

to go very fast

Example

The car sped down the highway.

Pattern
