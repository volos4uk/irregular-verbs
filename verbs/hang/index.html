Past tense of “hang”: hung, hung

The verb “hang” is irregular: its past simple is “hung” and its past participle is “hung”.

Base form (V1)
hang
/hæŋ/
Past simple (V2)
hung
/hʌŋ/
Past participle (V3)
hung
/hʌŋ/

Meaning

to fix something up so it does not touch the ground

Example

He hung his coat on the hook.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
