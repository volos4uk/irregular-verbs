Past tense of “steal”: stole, stolen

The verb “steal” is irregular: its past simple is “stole” and its past participle is “stolen”.

Base form (V1)
steal
/stiːl/
Past simple (V2)
stole
/stəʊl/
Past participle (V3)
stolen
/ˈstəʊlən/

Meaning

to take something that is not yours

Example

Someone stole my bicycle.

Pattern

ABC — all three forms are different
