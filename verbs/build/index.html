Past tense of “build”: built, built

The verb “build” is irregular: its past simple is “built” and its past participle is “built”.

Base form (V1)
build
/bɪld/
Past simple (V2)
built
/bɪlt/
Past participle (V3)
built
/bɪlt/

Meaning

to make something by putting parts together

Example

They built a new bridge last year.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
