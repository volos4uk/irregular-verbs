Past tense of “slay”: slew, slain

The verb “slay” is irregular: its past simple is “slew” and its past participle is “slain”.

Base form (V1)
slay
/sleɪ/
Past simple (V2)
slew
/sluː/
Past participle (V3)
slain
/sleɪn/

Meaning

to kill something, often in an old story

Example

The knight slew the dragon.
