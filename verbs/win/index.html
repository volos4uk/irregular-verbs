Past tense of “win”: won, won

The verb “win” is irregular: its past simple is “won” and its past participle is “won”.

Base form (V1)
win
/wɪn/
Past simple (V2)
won
/wʌn/
Past participle (V3)
won
/wʌn/

Meaning

to be the best in a game

Example

Our team won the championship.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
