Past tense of “begin”: began, begun

The verb “begin” is irregular: its past simple is “began” and its past participle is “begun”.

Base form (V1)
begin
/bɪˈɡɪn/
Past simple (V2)
began
/bɪˈɡæn/
Past participle (V3)
begun
/bɪˈɡʌn/

Meaning

to start

Example

The show began at eight.

Pattern

ABC — all three forms are different
