Past tense of “leap”: leapt, leapt

The verb “leap” is irregular: its past simple is “leapt” and its past participle is “leapt”.

Base form (V1)
leap
/liːp/
Past simple (V2)
leapt
/lɛpt/
Past participle (V3)
leapt
/lɛpt/

Meaning

to jump high or far

Example

The cat leapt onto the shelf.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
