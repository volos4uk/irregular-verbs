Past tense of “see”: saw, seen

The verb “see” is irregular: its past simple is “saw” and its past participle is “seen”.

Base form (V1)
see
/siː/
Past simple (V2)
saw
/sɔː/
Past participle (V3)
seen
/siːn/

Meaning

to notice something with your eyes

Example

I saw a shooting star last night.

Pattern
