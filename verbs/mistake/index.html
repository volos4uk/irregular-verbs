Past tense of “mistake”: mistook, mistaken

The verb “mistake” is irregular: its past simple is “mistook” and its past participle is “mistaken”.

Base form (V1)
mistake
/mɪˈsteɪk/
Past simple (V2)
mistook
/mɪˈstʊk/
Past participle (V3)
mistaken
/mɪˈsteɪkən/

Meaning

to think something wrong is right

Example

I mistook him for his brother.
