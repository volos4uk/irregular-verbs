Past tense of “creep”: crept, crept

The verb “creep” is irregular: its past simple is “crept” and its past participle is “crept”.

Base form (V1)
creep
/kriːp/
Past simple (V2)
crept
/krɛpt/
Past participle (V3)
crept
/krɛpt/

Meaning

to move slowly and quietly

Example

A spider crept across the wall.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
