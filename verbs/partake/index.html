Past tense of “partake”: partook, partaken

The verb “partake” is irregular: its past simple is “partook” and its past participle is “partaken”.

Base form (V1)
partake
/pɑːˈteɪk/
Past simple (V2)
partook
/ˈpɑːtʊk/
Past participle (V3)
partaken
/ˈpɑːteɪkən/

Meaning

to join in and do an activity

Example

They partook of the feast.
