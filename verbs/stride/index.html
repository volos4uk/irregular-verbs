Past tense of “stride”: strode, stridden

The verb “stride” is irregular: its past simple is “strode” and its past participle is “stridden”.

Base form (V1)
stride
/straɪd/
Past simple (V2)
strode
/strəʊd/
Past participle (V3)
stridden
/ˈstrɪdən/

Meaning

to walk with big steps

Example

She strode confidently into the room.
