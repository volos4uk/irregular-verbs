Past tense of “swing”: swung, swung

The verb “swing” is irregular: its past simple is “swung” and its past participle is “swung”.

Base form (V1)
swing
/swɪŋ/
Past simple (V2)
swung
/swʌŋ/
Past participle (V3)
swung
/swʌŋ/

Meaning

to move backward and forward

Example

The children swung on the old gate.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
