Past tense of “slink”: slunk, slunk

The verb “slink” is irregular: its past simple is “slunk” and its past participle is “slunk”.

Base form (V1)
slink
/slɪŋk/
Past simple (V2)
slunk
/slʌŋk/
Past participle (V3)
slunk
/slʌŋk/

Meaning

to move quietly because you feel shy or guilty

Example

The fox slunk into the bushes.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
