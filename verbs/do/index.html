Past tense of “do”: did, done

The verb “do” is irregular: its past simple is “did” and its past participle is “done”.

Base form (V1)
do
/duː/
Past simple (V2)
did
/dɪd/
Past participle (V3)
done
/dʌn/

Meaning

to perform an action

Example

She did her homework quickly.

Pattern

ABC — all three forms are different
