Past tense of “know”: knew, known

The verb “know” is irregular: its past simple is “knew” and its past participle is “known”.

Base form (V1)
know
/nəʊ/
Past simple (V2)
knew
/njuː/
Past participle (V3)
known
/nəʊn/

Meaning

to have information in your mind

Example

She knew the answer at once.
