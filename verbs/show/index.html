Past tense of “show”: showed, shown

The verb “show” is irregular: its past simple is “showed” and its past participle is “shown”.

Base form (V1)
show
/ʃəʊ/
Past simple (V2)
showed
/ʃəʊd/
Past participle (V3)
shown
/ʃəʊn/

Meaning

to let someone see something

Example

He showed us his new car.

Pattern
