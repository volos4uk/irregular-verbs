Past tense of “break”: broke, broken

The verb “break” is irregular: its past simple is “broke” and its past participle is “broken”.

Base form (V1)
break
/breɪk/
Past simple (V2)
broke
/brəʊk/
Past participle (V3)
broken
/ˈbrəʊkən/

Meaning

to make something come apart into pieces

Example

He broke the window with a stone.

Pattern
