Past tense of “shoot”: shot, shot

The verb “shoot” is irregular: its past simple is “shot” and its past participle is “shot”.

Base form (V1)
shoot
/ʃuːt/
Past simple (V2)
shot
/ʃɒt/
Past participle (V3)
shot
/ʃɒt/

Meaning

to fire a gun

Example

The hunter shot at the target.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
