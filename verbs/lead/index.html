Past tense of “lead”: led, led

The verb “lead” is irregular: its past simple is “led” and its past participle is “led”.

Base form (V1)
lead
/liːd/
Past simple (V2)
led
/lɛd/
Past participle (V3)
led
/lɛd/

Meaning

to go first and show others the way

Example

The guide led us through the forest.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
