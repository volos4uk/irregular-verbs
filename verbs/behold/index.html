Past tense of “behold”: beheld, beheld

The verb “behold” is irregular: its past simple is “beheld” and its past participle is “beheld”.

Base form (V1)
behold
/bɪˈhəʊld/
Past simple (V2)
beheld
/bɪˈhɛld/
Past participle (V3)
beheld
/bɪˈhɛld/

Meaning

to look at something amazing

Example

They beheld a stunning sunset.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
