Past tense of “find”: found, found

The verb “find” is irregular: its past simple is “found” and its past participle is “found”.

Base form (V1)
find
/faɪnd/
Past simple (V2)
found
/faʊnd/
Past participle (V3)
found
/faʊnd/

Meaning

to see something you were looking for

Example

They found the lost dog in the park.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
