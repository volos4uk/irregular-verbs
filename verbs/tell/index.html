Past tense of “tell”: told, told

The verb “tell” is irregular: its past simple is “told” and its past participle is “told”.

Base form (V1)
tell
/tɛl/
Past simple (V2)
told
/təʊld/
Past participle (V3)
told
/təʊld/

Meaning

to give someone information by speaking

Example

She told me an interesting story.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
