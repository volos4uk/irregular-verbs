Past tense of “swear”: swore, sworn

The verb “swear” is irregular: its past simple is “swore” and its past participle is “sworn”.

Base form (V1)
swear
/sweə/
Past simple (V2)
swore
/swɔː/
Past participle (V3)
sworn
/swɔːn/

Meaning

to make a strong promise, or say a bad word

Example

He swore to tell the truth.

Pattern

ABC — all three forms are different
