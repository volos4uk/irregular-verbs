Past tense of “swell”: swelled, swollen

The verb “swell” is irregular: its past simple is “swelled” and its past participle is “swollen”.

Base form (V1)
swell
/swɛl/
Past simple (V2)
swelled
/swɛld/
Past participle (V3)
swollen
/ˈswəʊlən/

Meaning

to become bigger, often after an injury

Example

His ankle swelled after the fall.

Pattern

ABC — all three forms are different
