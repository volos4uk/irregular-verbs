Past tense of “unbind”: unbound, unbound

The verb “unbind” is irregular: its past simple is “unbound” and its past participle is “unbound”.

Base form (V1)
unbind
/ʌˈnbaɪnd/
Past simple (V2)
unbound
/ʌˈnbaʊnd/
Past participle (V3)
unbound
/ʌˈnbaʊnd/

Meaning

to untie something

Example

She unbound the prisoner's hands.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
