Past tense of “undergo”: underwent, undergone

The verb “undergo” is irregular: its past simple is “underwent” and its past participle is “undergone”.

Base form (V1)
undergo
/ˌʌndəˈɡəʊ/
Past simple (V2)
underwent
/ˌʌndəˈwɛnt/
Past participle (V3)
undergone
/ˌʌndəˈɡɒn/

Meaning

to go through something hard, like an operation

Example

She underwent a long operation.

Pattern

ABC — all three forms are different
