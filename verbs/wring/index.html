Past tense of “wring”: wrung, wrung

The verb “wring” is irregular: its past simple is “wrung” and its past participle is “wrung”.

Base form (V1)
wring
/rɪŋ/
Past simple (V2)
wrung
/rʌŋ/
Past participle (V3)
wrung
/rʌŋ/

Meaning

to twist something to squeeze the water out

Example

She wrung the wet towel.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
