Past tense of “strive”: strove, striven

The verb “strive” is irregular: its past simple is “strove” and its past participle is “striven”.

Base form (V1)
strive
/straɪv/
Past simple (V2)
strove
/strəʊv/
Past participle (V3)
striven
/ˈstrɪvən/

Meaning

to try very hard

Example

They strove to finish on time.
