Past tense of “go”: went, gone

The verb “go” is irregular: its past simple is “went” and its past participle is “gone”.

Base form (V1)
go
/ɡəʊ/
Past simple (V2)
went
/wɛnt/
Past participle (V3)
gone
/ɡɒn/

Meaning

to move to another place

Example

We went to the cinema yesterday.

Pattern
