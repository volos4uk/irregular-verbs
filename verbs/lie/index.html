Past tense of “lie”: lay, lain

The verb “lie” is irregular: its past simple is “lay” and its past participle is “lain”.

Base form (V1)
lie
/laɪ/
Past simple (V2)
lay
/leɪ/
Past participle (V3)
lain
/leɪn/

Meaning

to rest your body flat, like on a bed (its past tense "lay" is spelled just like the verb "lay" — putting something down — but they're different words)

Example

The cat lay in the sun.

Pattern

ABC — all three forms are different
