Past tense of “have”: had, had

The verb “have” is irregular: its past simple is “had” and its past participle is “had”.

Base form (V1)
have
/hæv/
Past simple (V2)
had
/hæd/
Past participle (V3)
had
/hæd/

Meaning

to own or hold something

Example

We had a wonderful time at the beach.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
