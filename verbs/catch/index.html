Past tense of “catch”: caught, caught

The verb “catch” is irregular: its past simple is “caught” and its past participle is “caught”.

Base form (V1)
catch
/kætʃ/
Past simple (V2)
caught
/kɔːt/
Past participle (V3)
caught
/kɔːt/

Meaning

to grab something moving, like a ball

Example

The goalkeeper caught the ball easily.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
