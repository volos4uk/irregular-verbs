Past tense of “blow”: blew, blown

The verb “blow” is irregular: its past simple is “blew” and its past participle is “blown”.

Base form (V1)
blow
/bləʊ/
Past simple (V2)
blew
/bluː/
Past participle (V3)
blown

Meaning

to push air out of your mouth

Example
