Past tense of “interweave”: interwove, interwoven

The verb “interweave” is irregular: its past simple is “interwove” and its past participle is “interwoven”.

Base form (V1)
interweave
/ˌɪntəˈwiːv/
Past simple (V2)
interwove
/ˌɪntəˈwəʊv/
Past participle (V3)
interwoven
/ˌɪntəˈwəʊvən/

Meaning

to mix things together closely

Example

The author interwove two stories.
