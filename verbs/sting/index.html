Past tense of “sting”: stung, stung

The verb “sting” is irregular: its past simple is “stung” and its past participle is “stung”.

Base form (V1)
sting
/stɪŋ/
Past simple (V2)
stung
/stʌŋ/
Past participle (V3)
stung
/stʌŋ/

Meaning

to hurt someone with a small sharp part, like a bee

Example

A bee stung him on the arm.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
