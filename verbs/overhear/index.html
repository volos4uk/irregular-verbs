Past tense of “overhear”: overheard, overheard

The verb “overhear” is irregular: its past simple is “overheard” and its past participle is “overheard”.

Base form (V1)
overhear
/ˌəʊvəˈhiə/
Past simple (V2)
overheard
/ˌəʊvəˈhɜːd/
Past participle (V3)
overheard
/ˌəʊvəˈhɜːd/

Meaning

to hear people talking by accident

Example

She overheard their conversation.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
