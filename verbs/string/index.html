Past tense of “string”: strung, strung

The verb “string” is irregular: its past simple is “strung” and its past participle is “strung”.

Base form (V1)
string
/strɪŋ/
Past simple (V2)
strung
/strʌŋ/
Past participle (V3)
strung
/strʌŋ/

Meaning

to put small things on a line, one after another

Example

She strung the beads on a thread.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
