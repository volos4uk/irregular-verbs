Past tense of “feed”: fed, fed

The verb “feed” is irregular: its past simple is “fed” and its past participle is “fed”.

Base form (V1)
feed
/fiːd/
Past simple (V2)
fed
/fɛd/
Past participle (V3)
fed
/fɛd/

Meaning

to give food to someone or something

Example

She fed the cats before breakfast.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
