Past tense of “spring”: sprang, sprung

The verb “spring” is irregular: its past simple is “sprang” and its past participle is “sprung”.

Base form (V1)
spring
/sprɪŋ/
Past simple (V2)
sprang
/spræŋ/
Past participle (V3)
sprung
/sprʌŋ/

Meaning

to jump up fast

Example

The cat sprang from the chair.
