Past tense of “deal”: dealt, dealt

The verb “deal” is irregular: its past simple is “dealt” and its past participle is “dealt”.

Base form (V1)
deal
/diːl/
Past simple (V2)
dealt
/dɛlt/
Past participle (V3)
dealt
/dɛlt/

Meaning

to handle a problem, or give out cards in a game

Example

The teacher dealt with the problem calmly.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
