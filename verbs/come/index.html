Past tense of “come”: came, come

The verb “come” is irregular: its past simple is “came” and its past participle is “come”.

Base form (V1)
come
/kʌm/
Past simple (V2)
came
/keɪm/
Past participle (V3)
come
/kʌm/

Meaning

to move to where someone is

Example

They came home very late.

Pattern

ABA — base form and past participle are the same
