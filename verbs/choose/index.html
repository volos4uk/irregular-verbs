Past tense of “choose”: chose, chosen

The verb “choose” is irregular: its past simple is “chose” and its past participle is “chosen”.

Base form (V1)
choose
/tʃuːz/
Past simple (V2)
chose
/tʃəʊz/
Past participle (V3)
chosen
/ˈtʃəʊzən/

Meaning

to pick one thing out of several

Example

She chose the red dress.

Pattern

ABC — all three forms are different
