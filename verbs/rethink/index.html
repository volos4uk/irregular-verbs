Past tense of “rethink”: rethought, rethought

The verb “rethink” is irregular: its past simple is “rethought” and its past participle is “rethought”.

Base form (V1)
rethink
/riːˈθɪŋk/
Past simple (V2)
rethought
/rɪˈθɔːt/
Past participle (V3)
rethought
/rɪˈθɔːt/

Meaning

to think about something again

Example

We rethought our entire plan.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
