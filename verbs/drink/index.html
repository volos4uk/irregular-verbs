Past tense of “drink”: drank, drunk

The verb “drink” is irregular: its past simple is “drank” and its past participle is “drunk”.

Base form (V1)
drink
/drɪŋk/
Past simple (V2)
drank
/dræŋk/
Past participle (V3)
drunk
/drʌŋk/

Meaning

to swallow a liquid

Example

They drank tea in the garden.

Pattern

ABC — all three forms are different
