Past tense of “rebuild”: rebuilt, rebuilt

The verb “rebuild” is irregular: its past simple is “rebuilt” and its past participle is “rebuilt”.

Base form (V1)
rebuild
/rɪˈbɪld/
Past simple (V2)
rebuilt
/rɪˈbɪlt/
Past participle (V3)
rebuilt
/rɪˈbɪlt/

Meaning

to build something again

Example

They rebuilt the house after the fire.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
