Past tense of “dig”: dug, dug

The verb “dig” is irregular: its past simple is “dug” and its past participle is “dug”.

Base form (V1)
dig
/dɪɡ/
Past simple (V2)
dug
/dʌɡ/
Past participle (V3)
dug
/dʌɡ/

Meaning

to make a hole in the ground

Example

The workers dug a deep hole.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
