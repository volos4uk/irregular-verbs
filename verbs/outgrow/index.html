Past tense of “outgrow”: outgrew, outgrown

The verb “outgrow” is irregular: its past simple is “outgrew” and its past participle is “outgrown”.

Base form (V1)
outgrow
/aʊˈtɡrəʊ/
Past simple (V2)
outgrew
/aʊˈtɡruː/
Past participle (V3)
outgrown
/aʊˈtɡrəʊn/

Meaning

to get too big for something

Example

The boy outgrew his shoes quickly.
