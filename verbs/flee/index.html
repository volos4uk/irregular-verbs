Past tense of “flee”: fled, fled

The verb “flee” is irregular: its past simple is “fled” and its past participle is “fled”.

Base form (V1)
flee
/fliː/
Past simple (V2)
fled
/flɛd/
Past participle (V3)
fled
/flɛd/

Meaning

to run away from danger

Example

The villagers fled from the flood.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
