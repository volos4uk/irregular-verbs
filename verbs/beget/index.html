Past tense of “beget”: begot, begotten

The verb “beget” is irregular: its past simple is “begot” and its past participle is “begotten”.

Base form (V1)
beget
/bɪˈɡɛt/
Past simple (V2)
begot
/bɪˈɡɒt/
Past participle (V3)
begotten
/bɪˈɡɒtən/

Meaning

to be the reason something exists

Example

Greed begot more greed.

Pattern

ABC — all three forms are different
