Past tense of “swim”: swam, swum

The verb “swim” is irregular: its past simple is “swam” and its past participle is “swum”.

Base form (V1)
swim
/swɪm/
Past simple (V2)
swam
/swæm/
Past participle (V3)
swum
/swʌm/

Meaning

to move in water using your arms and legs

Example

They swam across the river.

Pattern

ABC — all three forms are different
