Past tense of “grind”: ground, ground

The verb “grind” is irregular: its past simple is “ground” and its past participle is “ground”.

Base form (V1)
grind
/ɡraɪnd/
Past simple (V2)
ground
/ɡraʊnd/
Past participle (V3)
ground
/ɡraʊnd/

Meaning

to break something into small pieces

Example

The machine ground the coffee beans.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
