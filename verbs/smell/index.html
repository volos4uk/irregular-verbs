Past tense of “smell”: smelt, smelt

The verb “smell” is irregular: its past simple is “smelt” and its past participle is “smelt”.

Base form (V1)
smell
/smɛl/
Past simple (V2)
smelt
/smɛlt/
Past participle (V3)
smelt
/smɛlt/

Meaning

to notice something with your nose

Example

The kitchen smelt of fresh bread.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
