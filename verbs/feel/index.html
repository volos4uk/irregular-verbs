Past tense of “feel”: felt, felt

The verb “feel” is irregular: its past simple is “felt” and its past participle is “felt”.

Base form (V1)
feel
/fiːl/
Past simple (V2)
felt
/fɛlt/
Past participle (V3)
felt
/fɛlt/

Meaning

to notice something in your body or heart

Example

I felt tired after the long trip.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
