Past tense of “breed”: bred, bred

The verb “breed” is irregular: its past simple is “bred” and its past participle is “bred”.

Base form (V1)
breed
/briːd/
Past simple (V2)
bred
/brɛd/
Past participle (V3)
bred
/brɛd/

Meaning

to have babies, like animals do

Example

They bred horses on the farm.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
