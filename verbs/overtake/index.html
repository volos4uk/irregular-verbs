Past tense of “overtake”: overtook, overtaken

The verb “overtake” is irregular: its past simple is “overtook” and its past participle is “overtaken”.

Base form (V1)
overtake
/ˌəʊvəˈteɪk/
Past simple (V2)
overtook
/ˌəʊvəˈtʊk/
Past participle (V3)
overtaken
/ˌəʊvəˈteɪkən/

Meaning

to pass someone who is in front of you

Example

The red car overtook us on the bridge.
