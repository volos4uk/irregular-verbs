Past tense of “lose”: lost, lost

The verb “lose” is irregular: its past simple is “lost” and its past participle is “lost”.

Base form (V1)
lose
/luːz/
Past simple (V2)
lost
/lɒst/
Past participle (V3)
lost
/lɒst/

Meaning

to not have something anymore

Example

He lost his wallet on the train.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
