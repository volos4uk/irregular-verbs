Past tense of “uphold”: upheld, upheld

The verb “uphold” is irregular: its past simple is “upheld” and its past participle is “upheld”.

Base form (V1)
uphold
/ʌˈphəʊld/
Past simple (V2)
upheld
/ʌˈphɛld/
Past participle (V3)
upheld
/ʌˈphɛld/

Meaning

to support a rule or a decision

Example

The court upheld the decision.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
