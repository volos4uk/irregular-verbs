Past tense of “lean”: leant, leant

The verb “lean” is irregular: its past simple is “leant” and its past participle is “leant”.

Base form (V1)
lean
/liːn/
Past simple (V2)
leant
/lɛnt/
Past participle (V3)
leant
/lɛnt/

Meaning

to put your body against something

Example

He leant against the wall.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
