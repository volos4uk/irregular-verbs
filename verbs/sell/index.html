Past tense of “sell”: sold, sold

The verb “sell” is irregular: its past simple is “sold” and its past participle is “sold”.

Base form (V1)
sell
/sɛl/
Past simple (V2)
sold
/səʊld/
Past participle (V3)
sold
/səʊld/

Meaning

to give something to someone for money

Example

They sold their house last spring.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
