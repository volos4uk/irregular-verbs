Past tense of “overthrow”: overthrew, overthrown

The verb “overthrow” is irregular: its past simple is “overthrew” and its past participle is “overthrown”.

Base form (V1)
overthrow
/ˌəʊvəˈθrəʊ/
Past simple (V2)
overthrew
/ˌəʊvəˈθruː/
Past participle (V3)
overthrown
/ˌəʊvəˈθrəʊn/

Meaning

to remove a leader by force

Example

The rebels overthrew the government.
