Past tense of “throw”: threw, thrown

The verb “throw” is irregular: its past simple is “threw” and its past participle is “thrown”.

Base form (V1)
throw
/θrəʊ/
Past simple (V2)
threw
/θruː/
Past participle (V3)
thrown
/θrəʊn/

Meaning

to send something through the air with your hand

Example

He threw the ball over the fence.

Pattern
