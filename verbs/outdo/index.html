Past tense of “outdo”: outdid, outdone

The verb “outdo” is irregular: its past simple is “outdid” and its past participle is “outdone”.

Base form (V1)
outdo
/aʊˈtduː/
Past simple (V2)
outdid
/aʊˈtdɪd/
Past participle (V3)
outdone
/aʊˈtdʌn/

Meaning

to do something better than someone else

Example

She outdid herself with the cake.
