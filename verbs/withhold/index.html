Past tense of “withhold”: withheld, withheld

The verb “withhold” is irregular: its past simple is “withheld” and its past participle is “withheld”.

Base form (V1)
withhold
/wɪˈðhəʊld/
Past simple (V2)
withheld
/wɪˈðhɛld/
Past participle (V3)
withheld
/wɪˈðhɛld/

Meaning

to not give something to someone

Example

They withheld the results for days.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
