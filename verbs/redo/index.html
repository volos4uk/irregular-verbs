Past tense of “redo”: redid, redone

The verb “redo” is irregular: its past simple is “redid” and its past participle is “redone”.

Base form (V1)
redo
/riːˈduː/
Past simple (V2)
redid
/rɪˈdɪd/
Past participle (V3)
redone
/riːˈdʌn/

Meaning

to do something again

Example

We had to redo the whole report.
