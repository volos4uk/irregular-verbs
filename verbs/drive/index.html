Past tense of “drive”: drove, driven

The verb “drive” is irregular: its past simple is “drove” and its past participle is “driven”.

Base form (V1)
drive
/draɪv/
Past simple (V2)
drove
/drəʊv/
Past participle (V3)
driven
/ˈdrɪvən/

Meaning

to control a car

Example

He drove all night.

Pattern
