Past tense of “sit”: sat, sat

The verb “sit” is irregular: its past simple is “sat” and its past participle is “sat”.

Base form (V1)
sit
/sɪt/
Past simple (V2)
sat
/sæt/
Past participle (V3)
sat
/sæt/

Meaning

to put your body down on a chair

Example

We sat by the window all evening.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
