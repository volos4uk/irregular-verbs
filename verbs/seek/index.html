Past tense of “seek”: sought, sought

The verb “seek” is irregular: its past simple is “sought” and its past participle is “sought”.

Base form (V1)
seek
/siːk/
Past simple (V2)
sought
/sɔːt/
Past participle (V3)
sought
/sɔːt/

Meaning

to look for something

Example

They sought help from a lawyer.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
