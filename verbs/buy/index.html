Past tense of “buy”: bought, bought

The verb “buy” is irregular: its past simple is “bought” and its past participle is “bought”.

Base form (V1)
buy
/baɪ/
Past simple (V2)
bought
/bɔːt/
Past participle (V3)
bought
/bɔːt/

Meaning

to get something by paying money

Example

He bought a car last week.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
