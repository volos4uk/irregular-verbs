Past tense of “undo”: undid, undone

The verb “undo” is irregular: its past simple is “undid” and its past participle is “undone”.

Base form (V1)
undo
/ʌˈnduː/
Past simple (V2)
undid
/ʌˈndɪd/
Past participle (V3)
undone
/ʌˈndʌn/

Meaning

to make something go back to how it was

Example

He undid the knot with his teeth.

Pattern

ABC — all three forms are different
