Past tense of “pay”: paid, paid

The verb “pay” is irregular: its past simple is “paid” and its past participle is “paid”.

Base form (V1)
pay
/peɪ/
Past simple (V2)
paid
/peɪd/
Past participle (V3)
paid
/peɪd/

Meaning

to give money for something

Example

He paid the bill with a card.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
