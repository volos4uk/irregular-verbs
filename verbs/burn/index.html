Past tense of “burn”: burnt, burnt

The verb “burn” is irregular: its past simple is “burnt” and its past participle is “burnt”.

Base form (V1)
burn
/bɜːn/
Past simple (V2)
burnt
/bɜːnt/
Past participle (V3)
burnt
/bɜːnt/

Meaning

to be on fire

Example

She burnt the toast this morning.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
