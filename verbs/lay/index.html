Past tense of “lay”: laid, laid

The verb “lay” is irregular: its past simple is “laid” and its past participle is “laid”.

Base form (V1)
lay
/leɪ/
Past simple (V2)
laid
/leɪd/
Past participle (V3)
laid
/leɪd/

Meaning

to put something down carefully (different from "lie" — resting your own body down)

Example

She laid the blanket on the grass.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
