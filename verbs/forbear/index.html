Past tense of “forbear”: forbore, forborne

The verb “forbear” is irregular: its past simple is “forbore” and its past participle is “forborne”.

Base form (V1)
forbear
/fɔːˈbeə/
Past simple (V2)
forbore
/fɔːˈbɔː/
Past participle (V3)
forborne
/fɔːˈbɔːn/

Meaning

to stop yourself from doing something

Example

He forbore to mention it.

Pattern

ABC — all three forms are different
