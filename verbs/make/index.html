Past tense of “make”: made, made

The verb “make” is irregular: its past simple is “made” and its past participle is “made”.

Base form (V1)
make
/meɪk/
Past simple (V2)
made
/meɪd/
Past participle (V3)
made
/meɪd/

Meaning

to build or create something

Example

She made a cake for his birthday.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
