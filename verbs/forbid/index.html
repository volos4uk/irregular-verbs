Past tense of “forbid”: forbade, forbidden

The verb “forbid” is irregular: its past simple is “forbade” and its past participle is “forbidden”.

Base form (V1)
forbid
/fəˈbɪd/
Past simple (V2)
forbade
/fəˈbeɪd/
Past participle (V3)
forbidden
/fəˈbɪdən/

Meaning

to say someone must not do something

Example

The teacher forbade talking in class.
