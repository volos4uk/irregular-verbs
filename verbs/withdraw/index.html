Past tense of “withdraw”: withdrew, withdrawn

The verb “withdraw” is irregular: its past simple is “withdrew” and its past participle is “withdrawn”.

Base form (V1)
withdraw
/wɪˈðdrɔː/
Past simple (V2)
withdrew
/wɪˈðdruː/
Past participle (V3)
withdrawn
/wɪˈðdrɔːn/

Meaning

to take money out of the bank, or to move back

Example

She withdrew some money from the bank.

Pattern

ABC — all three forms are different
