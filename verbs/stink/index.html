Past tense of “stink”: stank, stunk

The verb “stink” is irregular: its past simple is “stank” and its past participle is “stunk”.

Base form (V1)
stink
/stɪŋk/
Past simple (V2)
stank
/stæŋk/
Past participle (V3)
stunk
/stʌŋk/

Meaning

to smell very bad

Example

The garbage stank in the heat.

Pattern

ABC — all three forms are different
