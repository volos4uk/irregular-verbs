Past tense of “spoil”: spoilt, spoilt

The verb “spoil” is irregular: its past simple is “spoilt” and its past participle is “spoilt”.

Base form (V1)
spoil
/spɔɪl/
Past simple (V2)
spoilt
/spɔɪlt/
Past participle (V3)
spoilt
/spɔɪlt/

Meaning

to make something bad or ruined

Example

The rain spoilt our picnic.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
