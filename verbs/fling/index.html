Past tense of “fling”: flung, flung

The verb “fling” is irregular: its past simple is “flung” and its past participle is “flung”.

Base form (V1)
fling
/flɪŋ/
Past simple (V2)
flung
/flʌŋ/
Past participle (V3)
flung
/flʌŋ/

Meaning

to throw something fast, without care

Example

He flung the door open angrily.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
