Past tense of “understand”: understood, understood

The verb “understand” is irregular: its past simple is “understood” and its past participle is “understood”.

Base form (V1)
understand
/ˌʌndəˈstænd/
Past simple (V2)
understood
/ˌʌndəˈstʊd/
Past participle (V3)
understood
/ˌʌndəˈstʊd/

Meaning

to know what something means

Example

I understood the lesson completely.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
