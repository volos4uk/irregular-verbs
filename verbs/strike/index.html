Past tense of “strike”: struck, struck

The verb “strike” is irregular: its past simple is “struck” and its past participle is “struck”.

Base form (V1)
strike
/straɪk/
Past simple (V2)
struck
/strʌk/
Past participle (V3)
struck
/strʌk/

Meaning

to hit something hard

Example

The clock struck midnight.

Pattern
