Past tense of “stand”: stood, stood

The verb “stand” is irregular: its past simple is “stood” and its past participle is “stood”.

Base form (V1)
stand
/stænd/
Past simple (V2)
stood
/stʊd/
Past participle (V3)
stood
/stʊd/

Meaning

to be on your feet

Example

They stood in line for two hours.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
