Past tense of “wind”: wound, wound

The verb “wind” is irregular: its past simple is “wound” and its past participle is “wound”.

Base form (V1)
wind
/waɪnd/
Past simple (V2)
wound
/waʊnd/
Past participle (V3)
wound
/waʊnd/

Meaning

to turn something around and around

Example

He wound the old clock every night.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
