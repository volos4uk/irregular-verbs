Past tense of “become”: became, become

The verb “become” is irregular: its past simple is “became” and its past participle is “become”.

Base form (V1)
become
/bɪˈkʌm/
Past simple (V2)
became
/bɪˈkeɪm/
Past participle (V3)
become
/bɪˈkʌm/

Meaning

to start to be something new

Example

She became a doctor last year.

Pattern

ABA — base form and past participle are the same
