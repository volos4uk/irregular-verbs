Past tense of “cling”: clung, clung

The verb “cling” is irregular: its past simple is “clung” and its past participle is “clung”.

Base form (V1)
cling
/klɪŋ/
Past simple (V2)
clung
/klʌŋ/
Past participle (V3)
clung
/klʌŋ/

Meaning

to hold on tightly

Example

The child clung to her mother.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
