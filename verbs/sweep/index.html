Past tense of “sweep”: swept, swept

The verb “sweep” is irregular: its past simple is “swept” and its past participle is “swept”.

Base form (V1)
sweep
/swiːp/
Past simple (V2)
swept
/swɛpt/
Past participle (V3)
swept
/swɛpt/

Meaning

to clean the floor with a broom

Example

She swept the floor this morning.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
