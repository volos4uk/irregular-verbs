Past tense of “remake”: remade, remade

The verb “remake” is irregular: its past simple is “remade” and its past participle is “remade”.

Base form (V1)
remake
/riːˈmeɪk/
Past simple (V2)
remade
/rɪˈmeɪd/
Past participle (V3)
remade
/rɪˈmeɪd/

Meaning

to make a new version of something old

Example

The studio remade the classic film.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
