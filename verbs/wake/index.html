Past tense of “wake”: woke, woken

The verb “wake” is irregular: its past simple is “woke” and its past participle is “woken”.

Base form (V1)
wake
/weɪk/
Past simple (V2)
woke
/wəʊk/
Past participle (V3)
woken
/ˈwəʊkən/

Meaning

to stop sleeping

Example

I woke up at five o'clock.

Pattern

ABC — all three forms are different
