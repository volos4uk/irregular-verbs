Past tense of “eat”: ate, eaten

The verb “eat” is irregular: its past simple is “ate” and its past participle is “eaten”.

Base form (V1)
eat
/iːt/
Past simple (V2)
ate
/eɪt/
Past participle (V3)
eaten
/ˈiːtən/

Meaning

to put food in your mouth and swallow it

Example

He ate the whole pizza.
