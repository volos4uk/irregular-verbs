Past tense of “bear”: bore, borne

The verb “bear” is irregular: its past simple is “bore” and its past participle is “borne”.

Base form (V1)
bear
/beə/
Past simple (V2)
bore
/bɔː/
Past participle (V3)
borne
/bɔːn/

Meaning

to carry something heavy, or to accept pain

Example

She bore the pain silently.

Pattern

ABC — all three forms are different
