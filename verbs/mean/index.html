Past tense of “mean”: meant, meant

The verb “mean” is irregular: its past simple is “meant” and its past participle is “meant”.

Base form (V1)
mean
/miːn/
Past simple (V2)
meant
/mɛnt/
Past participle (V3)
meant
/mɛnt/

Meaning

to have a certain meaning

Example

I never meant to hurt you.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
