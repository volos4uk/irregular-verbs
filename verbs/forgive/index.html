Past tense of “forgive”: forgave, forgiven

The verb “forgive” is irregular: its past simple is “forgave” and its past participle is “forgiven”.

Base form (V1)
forgive
/fəˈɡɪv/
Past simple (V2)
forgave
/fəˈɡeɪv/
Past participle (V3)
forgiven
/fəˈɡɪvən/

Meaning

to stop being angry at someone

Example

She forgave him for the mistake.

Pattern

ABC — all three forms are different
