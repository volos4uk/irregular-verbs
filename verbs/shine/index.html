Past tense of “shine”: shone, shone

The verb “shine” is irregular: its past simple is “shone” and its past participle is “shone”.

Base form (V1)
shine
/ʃaɪn/
Past simple (V2)

Past participle (V3)
shone

Meaning

to give off bright light

Example

Pattern
