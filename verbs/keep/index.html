Past tense of “keep”: kept, kept

The verb “keep” is irregular: its past simple is “kept” and its past participle is “kept”.

Base form (V1)
keep
/kiːp/
Past simple (V2)
kept
/kɛpt/
Past participle (V3)
kept
/kɛpt/

Meaning

to hold onto something and not let it go

Example

He kept the secret for years.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
