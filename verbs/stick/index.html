Past tense of “stick”: stuck, stuck

The verb “stick” is irregular: its past simple is “stuck” and its past participle is “stuck”.

Base form (V1)
stick
/stɪk/
Past simple (V2)
stuck
/stʌk/
Past participle (V3)
stuck
/stʌk/

Meaning

to make one thing stay on another thing

Example

The stamp stuck to my fingers.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
