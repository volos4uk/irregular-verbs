Past tense of “spill”: spilt, spilt

The verb “spill” is irregular: its past simple is “spilt” and its past participle is “spilt”.

Base form (V1)
spill
/spɪl/
Past simple (V2)
spilt
/spɪlt/
Past participle (V3)
spilt
/spɪlt/

Meaning

to let a liquid fall out by accident

Example

I spilt coffee on my shirt.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
