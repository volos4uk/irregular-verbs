Past tense of “send”: sent, sent

The verb “send” is irregular: its past simple is “sent” and its past participle is “sent”.

Base form (V1)
send
/sɛnd/
Past simple (V2)
sent
/sɛnt/
Past participle (V3)
sent
/sɛnt/

Meaning

to make something go to another place

Example

I sent the email an hour ago.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
