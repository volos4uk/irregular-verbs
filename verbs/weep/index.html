Past tense of “weep”: wept, wept

The verb “weep” is irregular: its past simple is “wept” and its past participle is “wept”.

Base form (V1)
weep
/wiːp/
Past simple (V2)
wept
/wɛpt/
Past participle (V3)
wept
/wɛpt/

Meaning

to cry

Example

The child wept when she lost her toy.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
