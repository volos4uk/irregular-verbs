Past tense of “withstand”: withstood, withstood

The verb “withstand” is irregular: its past simple is “withstood” and its past participle is “withstood”.

Base form (V1)
withstand
/wɪˈðstænd/
Past simple (V2)
withstood
/wɪˈðstʊd/
Past participle (V3)
withstood
/wɪˈðstʊd/

Meaning

to stay strong when something pushes against you

Example

The wall withstood the storm.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
