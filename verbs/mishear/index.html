Past tense of “mishear”: misheard, misheard

The verb “mishear” is irregular: its past simple is “misheard” and its past participle is “misheard”.

Base form (V1)
mishear
/mɪˈshɪə/
Past simple (V2)
misheard
/mɪˈshɜːd/
Past participle (V3)
misheard
/mɪˈshɜːd/

Meaning

to hear something the wrong way

Example

I misheard the address.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
