Past tense of “misunderstand”: misunderstood, misunderstood

The verb “misunderstand” is irregular: its past simple is “misunderstood” and its past participle is “misunderstood”.

Base form (V1)
misunderstand
/mɪˌsʌndəˈstænd/
Past simple (V2)
misunderstood
/mɪˌsʌndəˈstʊd/
Past participle (V3)
misunderstood
/mɪˌsʌndəˈstʊd/

Meaning

to understand something the wrong way

Example

She misunderstood my question.

Pattern

ABB — past simple and past participle are the same
