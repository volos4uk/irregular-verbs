Past tense of “tear”: tore, torn

The verb “tear” is irregular: its past simple is “tore” and its past participle is “torn”.

Base form (V1)
tear
/teə/
Past simple (V2)
tore
/tɔː/
Past participle (V3)
torn
/tɔːn/

Meaning

to pull something apart

Example

He tore the paper in half.
