Past tense of “ring”: rang, rung

The verb “ring” is irregular: its past simple is “rang” and its past participle is “rung”.

Base form (V1)
ring
/rɪŋ/
Past simple (V2)
rang
/ræŋ/
Past participle (V3)
rung
/rʌŋ/

Meaning

to make a sound like a bell

Example

The phone rang twice.
