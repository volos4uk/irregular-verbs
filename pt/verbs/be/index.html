Passado e particípio de “be”: was / were, been

O verbo “be” (ser) é irregular: o passado simples é “was / were” e o particípio é “been”.

Infinitivo (V1)
be
/biː/
Passado simples (V2)
was / were
/wɒz/
Particípio (V3)
been
/biːn/

Significado

ser

to exist, or to have a certain quality

Exemplo

They were happy at the party.
