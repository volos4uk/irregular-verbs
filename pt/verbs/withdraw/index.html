Passado e particípio de “withdraw”: withdrew, withdrawn

O verbo “withdraw” (retirar) é irregular: o passado simples é “withdrew” e o particípio é “withdrawn”.

Infinitivo (V1)
withdraw
/wɪˈðdrɔː/
Passado simples (V2)
withdrew
/wɪˈðdruː/
Particípio (V3)
withdrawn
/wɪˈðdrɔːn/

Significado

retirar

to take money out of the bank, or to move back

Exemplo

She withdrew some money from the bank.
