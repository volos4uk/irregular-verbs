Động từ “be”: be – was / were – been

Động từ “be” (là / thì) là động từ bất quy tắc: cột 2 là “was / were”, cột 3 là “been”.

Cột 1 (V1)
be
/biː/
Cột 2 (V2)
was / were
/wɒz/
Cột 3 (V3)
been
/biːn/

Nghĩa

là / thì

to exist, or to have a certain quality

Ví dụ

They were happy at the party.
