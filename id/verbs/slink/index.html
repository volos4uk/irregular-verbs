Verb 1, 2, 3 dari “slink”: slink – slunk – slunk

Kata kerja “slink” (menyelinap) tidak beraturan: verb 2-nya “slunk” dan verb 3-nya “slunk”.

slink
/slɪŋk/
Verb 2 (V2)
slunk
/slʌŋk/
Verb 3 (V3)
slunk
/slʌŋk/

Arti

menyelinap

to move quietly because you feel shy or guilty

Contoh

The fox slunk into the bushes.

Pola
