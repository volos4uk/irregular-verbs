Verb 1, 2, 3 dari “lie”: lie – lay – lain

Kata kerja “lie” (berbaring) tidak beraturan: verb 2-nya “lay” dan verb 3-nya “lain”.

Verb 1 (V1)
lie
/laɪ/
Verb 2 (V2)
lay
/leɪ/
Verb 3 (V3)
lain
/leɪn/

Arti

berbaring

to rest your body flat, like on a bed (its past tense "lay" is spelled just like the verb "lay" — putting something down — but they're different words)

Contoh

The cat lay in the sun.

Pola

ABC — ketiga bentuk berbeda
