Verb 1, 2, 3 dari “show”: show – showed – shown

Kata kerja “show” (menunjukkan) tidak beraturan: verb 2-nya “showed” dan verb 3-nya “shown”.

Verb 1 (V1)
show
/ʃəʊ/
Verb 2 (V2)
showed
/ʃəʊd/
Verb 3 (V3)
shown
/ʃəʊn/

Arti

menunjukkan

to let someone see something

Contoh

He showed us his new car.
