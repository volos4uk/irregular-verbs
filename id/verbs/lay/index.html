Verb 1, 2, 3 dari “lay”: lay – laid – laid

Kata kerja “lay” (meletakkan) tidak beraturan: verb 2-nya “laid” dan verb 3-nya “laid”.

Verb 1 (V1)
lay
/leɪ/
Verb 2 (V2)
laid
/leɪd/
Verb 3 (V3)
laid
/leɪd/

Arti

meletakkan

to put something down carefully (different from "lie" — resting your own body down)

Contoh

She laid the blanket on the grass.

Pola

ABB — verb 2 dan verb 3 sama
